All indices

How one index is made.

The method is public and the same for every GPU. Here it is on the H100 SXM index, using the prices that went into it.

  1. One vote per cloud

    Each cloud counts once, at its lowest listed price. A cloud with forty listings can't outvote a cloud with one.

  2. Bad prints held out

    A price more than three times the median, or under a third of it, is set aside, as is a feed that is failing.

  3. A trimmed average

    With five or more clouds, the top and bottom fifth are cut and the rest averaged. Under three clouds, nothing is published.

  4. Chain-linked

    When a cloud joins or leaves, the index moves only by how prices changed, not by who is in it. That is why the index can differ from this hour's plain average.

  5. Fixed and hashed

    For settlement indices, each hour is fixed five hours later and chained to the hour before by hash. Anyone can recompute one.
