FAQs

Questions we hear before the first invoice

How does billing work?

You buy credits, and usage draws them down: per token for Serverless, per GPU-second for On Demand, and per token or GPU-hour for training.

Can I cap what I spend?

Yes. Set a monthly spend limit on your account. Auto Reload is separate: it buys more credits when your balance runs low, so production keeps running, and it never raises your limit.

How is prompt caching billed?

Caching is automatic, with no setup and no write fee. Cached input bills at 50% off by default, with deeper discounts on select models.

What happens when we go over our reservation?

We customize inference to your needs. Usage above your reservation can bill at standard Serverless rates, never a penalty rate. Overage isn’t covered by the SLA and follows your Serverless rate limits.

When should we move from Serverless to On Demand?

When utilization is high, when rate limits get in your way, or when you want to serve a model that isn’t on Serverless, including one you trained. On Demand bills per GPU-second, so a busy deployment usually costs less per token.

Does serving a trained model cost extra?

No. You pay for the deployment by the GPU-second, plus the cost of the training job.

How is Fireworks Sovereign priced?

By contract. Sovereign is available in preview to Enterprise customers. Talk to us to scope your environment.
